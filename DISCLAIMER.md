## Personal use

Riggles to Bricks is for building your own riggle, for yourself. Please don't sell the models, the instructions,
or kits made from them.

Riggles is an onchain collection: each riggle is drawn by one engine from one 32 byte hash fixed at mint. The
site redraws your riggle in your browser from that hash, with that engine, to design a brick model of it. The
hashes were read from the Riggles API once, when the site was built, and are included in the site. Nothing is
fetched while you use it.
