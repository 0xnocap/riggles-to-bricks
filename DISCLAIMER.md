## Personal use

Squiggle to Bricks is for building your own Chromie Squiggle, for yourself. Please don't sell the models, the instructions, or kits made from them.

Chromie Squiggle is an artwork by Snowfro, published by Art Blocks. The site reads a Squiggle's public data (its hash and features) and its generator script from Art Blocks' public endpoints, and redraws it in your browser to design a brick model of it. Art Blocks' pictures of the Squiggles are loaded from Art Blocks, not hosted here.
